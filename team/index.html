How he works

How Kirill approaches your account.

No secret sauce. The same steps, in the same order, so nothing important gets skipped.

He starts with your Business Profile

Your primary category decides which searches you can show up for. If an HVAC company picks the wrong one, "furnace repair Stoney Creek" is an uphill fight before the website even comes into it.

He measures from where your customers are

Rankings are checked across a grid of points in your service area, not from one spot. A plumber can be first in the lower city and missing on the Mountain. You'd never know from a single check.

He trusts Search Console over estimates

Keyword tools model traffic. Search Console measures it. We've seen a tool estimate a site's traffic at about five times what Search Console showed, so the real numbers come first.

He gives every service its own page

"Drain cleaning Hamilton" and "sump pump installation Ancaster" are different searches from different people. One page trying to rank for both usually ranks for neither.

He protects what already works

Before anything changes, he checks what ranks today. We've seen pages deleted with no redirect, including one that ranked for a search with real monthly volume. That's an avoidable loss.

He explains it plainly

Every fix comes with the reason, in a sentence or two. If something didn't work, the report says so.

Ground rules

What Kirill won't do.

Sometimes the clearest way to explain how someone works is to say what they refuse to do.

  • Promise you a #1 ranking. Google says nobody can guarantee one.
  • Stuff keywords into your business name or invent an address. Both break Google's Business Profile guidelines and put your listing at risk.
  • Report vanity keywords. The searches we track are the ones that lead to calls, like "emergency plumber Hamilton Mountain", not hundreds of terms nobody types.
  • Name your business on this website. Every example here is anonymized. Your results are yours.
  • Pretend to be down the street. We're in Etobicoke and we work with Hamilton businesses remotely. That's the whole story.
