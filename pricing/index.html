What moves the number

Why two quotes can look so different.

Two plumbers in Hamilton can get very different SEO quotes, and both can be fair. These are the things that change the price.

How many services you sell

We build one page per service and intent. A company doing drain cleaning, water heaters and sump pumps needs more pages than one that only cleans drains.

How much ground you cover

A shop that only serves the Mountain needs less than a company working from Grimsby to Waterdown. More areas means more tracking and more local pages.

How much is broken today

A site with robots.txt blocking Google, or nine of ten service pages missing a main heading, needs repair before it can grow. We've seen both.

How crowded your search is

Some searches have far more businesses fighting for them than others. "Emergency plumber Hamilton" is a busier fight than a niche service with a handful of local providers.

Who writes the pages

Fixing pages you already have costs less than writing new ones from scratch. Your own photos and project details help, too.

Project or ongoing

An audit or a website has an end date. Tracking, maintenance and posts don't. We price each one the way the work is actually done.

Monthly local SEO

What a month of work is for.

A monthly plan shouldn't be a mystery box. Here's what the money pays for, so you can hold us to it.

  • Geo-grid rank tracking for the searches that lead to calls, across your service area.
  • Business Profile upkeep: categories, services, photos, posts and reviews.
  • New or improved service pages, at a pace we agree on before we start.
  • A click-through audit in Search Console. Pages ranking 4–10 with weak clicks get new titles, and we recheck them after 30 days.
  • Defensive checks for spam links, deleted pages and broken redirects.
  • A plain-English report: calls, leads, rankings, what we did and what didn't work.

Before you sign anything

Your proposal sets out the contract length and notice period, so you see both before you agree to anything.

Whoever you hire, ask for the terms in writing before you start. That includes us.

Questions to ask an SEO company before you sign

Cheap SEO

What cheap SEO usually leaves out.

A low price isn't a red flag by itself. What's missing from the work is. These gaps come straight from audits we've run.

Tracking from more than one spot

The map pack moves with the searcher. A report that checks rankings from one address can say "#1" while customers in Dundas never see you.

Conversion tracking

We've seen Google Ads accounts running with no conversion tracking at all. Nobody could tell which clicks turned into calls.

Pages written for your business

We've found service pages naming a different Ontario city, and a fake phone number from a cloned template on 19 pages. That's what copy-and-paste SEO looks like.

Redirects

We've seen pages deleted with no redirect, including one that ranked for a search with real monthly volume. The ranking went with it.

Honest numbers

We've seen a keyword tool estimate a site's traffic at about five times what Search Console showed. Reports built on estimates look great and mean little.

FAQ

Pricing questions we hear a lot.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. We look at your website, your Business Profile and your current rankings, then send the three fixes that matter most. You can act on them yourself. There's no obligation to hire us.

Does the Google Ads fee include my ad spend?

No. They're two separate numbers. Ad spend is what Google charges for clicks. The management fee is what we charge to run the account. Any quote you get should show both clearly.

Can I start with just one service?

Yes. If your Business Profile is the problem, fix that first. If your website is the problem, start there. The free audit tells you which one is costing you calls.

Do I pay extra for AI search optimization?

For most local businesses, AI search work overlaps with good SEO: structured data, consistent business details and clear answers. Be wary of anyone selling it as a bolt-on with big promises.

How long do I have to commit for?

That's set out in your proposal, with the notice period, before any work starts. Whoever you hire, get it in writing.
