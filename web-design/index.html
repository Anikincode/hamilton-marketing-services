The problem

Most local websites are built to look good, not to be found.

A beautiful website nobody finds is a brochure sitting in a drawer.

About 590 people a month search "web design hamilton". What they really want is a site that brings in calls.

Here's the problem. Most small business sites are designed first and "optimized" later, if ever. Every service ends up on one page. It looks fine, but Google has nothing clear to rank.

A plumber on Hamilton Mountain doesn't need more animation. They need a page Google can match to "emergency plumber Hamilton Mountain", and a phone number that's easy to tap.

Already have a site that isn't showing up? It may not need replacing. Try technical SEO fixes first.

What makes a site rank

What makes a website rank in Hamilton?

Design matters to the people who visit. These six things decide whether Google sends them to you in the first place.

One page per service

"Furnace repair Stoney Creek" and "AC installation Hamilton" are different searches. Give each its own page. One page trying to rank for both usually ranks for neither.

The right city in the right places

Title, main heading and opening lines name the service and the area. A kitchen renovation page aimed at Ancaster should say Ancaster, not the city the template came from.

One clear heading per page

Every page gets one H1 that says what it's about. Big bold text that isn't tagged as a heading doesn't count.

Fast on a phone

Google looks at the mobile version of your site first. Someone with a flooded basement in Dundas won't wait for a slow page.

Open to Google

One line in robots.txt can tell Google to ignore the whole site. We've seen it on a live site, so we check indexing before and after launch.

Easy for AI to quote

Structured data, plain answers and the same business details everywhere help Google and AI assistants understand who you are.

How we build

Our web design process, step by step.

Search comes first, then design. That way nothing has to be patched in after launch.

  1. Check your Business Profile and your market

    We start with your Google Business Profile categories, because the primary category decides which searches you can show for on Maps. Then we see what the sites ranking in Hamilton cover that yours doesn't.

  2. Map searches to pages

    We group keywords by intent and collect the "People also ask" questions. "Kitchen renovation Ancaster" and "basement waterproofing cost" end up on different pages, each with one job.

  3. Write the pages

    Every service page follows a set formula: what you do, where, what drives the price, why you, and the questions people ask before they call.

  4. Design and build

    We build on Wix or as a fast static site. Mobile layout first, a phone number you can tap, and a quote form that works on a small screen.

  5. Technical checks before launch

    Titles, headings, structured data, sitemap, robots.txt and a speed test in PageSpeed Insights. On a redesign, every old URL already has a new home.

  6. Launch and watch

    We submit the sitemap in Google Search Console, watch which pages get indexed, and track rankings across your service area with geo-grid tracking. Then a monthly check rewrites titles on pages ranking 4–10 with weak clicks.

Redesigns

Redesigning your website without losing your rankings.

A redesign is like moving house. If you don't forward your mail, the letters stop arriving. Google is the mail carrier.

Your old pages have built up trust. Delete or rename them without a redirect, and Google hits a dead end.

We've audited a site that deleted pages with no redirect, including one that ranked for a search with real monthly volume. That traffic didn't follow to the new site. It just stopped.

The full walkthrough is in our guide: how to redesign a website without losing rankings.

Our redesign checklist

  • List every old URL, from a crawl and from Search Console, and note which ones bring clicks today.
  • Keep the URLs that work. Change one only for a good reason.
  • Point every old URL at its closest new page with a permanent (301) redirect.
  • Carry over the titles and content that rank, then improve them.
  • Build on a hidden preview, and make sure the "don't index" setting comes off at launch.
  • Watch Search Console for broken pages in the weeks after launch.

FAQ

Web design questions from Hamilton businesses.

How much does a website cost in Hamilton?

It depends on the number of pages, the platform, and whether you need copy and photos. We quote once we know those. The free audit tells you whether you need a new site at all, or just fixes to the one you have.

How long does a new website take?

The biggest factor is content. If your service details and photos are ready, everything moves faster.

Will my new website rank on Google straight away?

Nobody can promise that, and Google says so itself. A new site built properly gets indexed and starts showing for searches over the following months.

Will a redesign hurt my rankings?

It can, if old pages are deleted or renamed without redirects. That's the classic way redesigns lose traffic. We map every old URL before launch so Google follows you to the new pages.

Can you fix my existing WordPress site instead of rebuilding it?

Yes. We build new sites on Wix or as static sites, and work on existing WordPress sites. If yours is sound, fixing it is often cheaper than replacing it.
