Who it's for

When you need an SEO consultant, not a full agency.

Some businesses don't need another pair of hands. They need a map.

Maybe your office manager runs the Google Business Profile. Maybe a developer looks after the website, or a marketing coordinator writes the blog. They're capable. They just don't know which SEO jobs matter most, or in what order.

That's where an SEO consultant fits. We set the direction and check the work. Your people do it.

You have someone in-house

A marketing person or web developer who can make changes, but needs direction and a second set of eyes.

You're not sure your agency is working

Reports look busy, but calls haven't moved. You want an independent read before you renew.

You're planning a redesign

You want the URL map and redirect plan checked before launch, not after the rankings drop.

You want a plan before you spend

Which services need pages, which areas to target, and what comes first. Does "roof repair Ancaster" deserve its own page?

What you get

What our SEO consulting covers.

It follows the same Core 30 method behind all our work. Your team just does the hands-on part.

  1. A full audit

    Website, Google Business Profile and current rankings. Every problem gets ranked by how much it's likely costing you, so your team knows where to start.

  2. Business Profile review

    We start with categories. The primary category decides which searches you can show for on Maps, and it's easy to get wrong.

  3. A keyword and page plan

    We cluster your keywords by intent, mine the "People also ask" questions, and map one page to each service and intent. Your team gets a build list, in order.

  4. Change reviews

    Before your developer renames a URL, merges pages or deletes old content, we check it. Careless edits quietly cost rankings. Catching them is what defensive SEO is for.

  5. Check-ins and training

    Regular calls to review Search Console and what shipped. We also show your team how to write a title for one specific search and spot problems early.

Want the full picture? Our SEO methodology walks through the Core 30 framework step by step, and our SEO audit page shows what the audit checks.

Second opinions

A second opinion on your SEO, from someone with no stake in it.

Getting a second opinion on SEO is like asking another mechanic before a big repair. Sometimes the first quote was right. Sometimes it wasn't.

Here's what we check when a Hamilton business asks us to look over its current SEO:

  • Do the numbers match Search Console? We've seen a keyword tool estimate a site's traffic about five times higher than Search Console showed. Tools model. Search Console measures.
  • Are the tracked keywords real searches? A long list of terms nobody types looks impressive and means nothing. "Plumber Hamilton Mountain" matters. Vanity phrases don't.
  • Has anything shipped? We've audited a business with 9 of 10 service pages missing an H1, still unfixed after four audits. Reports aren't progress.
  • Are the links safe? One small business site we looked at had a network of 60 spam domains pointing links at it. We built a disavow file.

Reviewing an SEO proposal?

Before you sign with any SEO company, check what's actually included, who does the work, and how results get measured.

A good proposal names specific pages and specific searches. A weak one promises "more traffic" and lists tasks without saying why.

How we think

What an SEO expert should actually tell you.

Good advice is specific. It names the page, the problem and the fix.

Here's a real example from our own site. Search Console showed a page sitting at average position 3–5 with zero clicks from 233 impressions.

The lazy advice would be "we need to rank higher". But the page was already near the top. People saw it and didn't click.

That's a title problem, not a ranking problem. The fix is a better title and description, then a recheck after 30 days. It's the same monthly click-through audit that's part of our method.

Your team can learn to spot this too. Our guide to Google Search Console for business owners shows where to look.

Google itself says nobody can guarantee a #1 ranking. If a consultant promises one, keep looking. An expert should show you evidence, explain it in plain English, and tell you when something didn't work.

Working together

How consulting works, and what it costs.

We work by phone, email and video call. We're based in Etobicoke, so there's no Hamilton office to visit, and you don't need one.

SEO consulting is usually sold in one of three ways. Each suits a different situation.

One-off audit and plan

A full review and a prioritized action list your team works through. Good before a redesign, or when you're starting from scratch.

Hourly advice

For questions, change reviews and second opinions as they come up. Good when your team is experienced and just needs a sounding board.

Monthly advisory

A standing check-in, a Search Console review and next month's priorities. Good when someone in-house works on SEO every week.

FAQ

Questions about hiring an SEO consultant.

What's the difference between an SEO consultant and an SEO agency?

A consultant advises and your team does the work. An agency does the work for you. Consulting suits businesses with someone in-house. Full service suits owners with no time or staff.

Can you work alongside our web developer or current agency?

Yes. We can review their changes, check their reports against Search Console, and give your developer a clear task list. The goal is better work, not finger-pointing.

Can you train our staff to do SEO?

Yes. We focus on what they'll actually use: Search Console, the Business Profile, writing titles for a specific search, and spotting problems before they cost rankings.

How much does an SEO consultant cost in Hamilton?

It depends on the format: a one-off audit, hourly advice or a monthly advisory. Our rates are consulting formats offered and rates. The free audit is a sensible first step either way.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. We work with Hamilton businesses by phone, email and video call. We're based in Etobicoke, and we don't pretend to have a Hamilton office.

What should an SEO expert never promise?

A guaranteed #1 ranking. Google says nobody can guarantee one. Be wary of anyone who does, or who can't show you their results in Search Console.
