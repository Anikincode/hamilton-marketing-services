Honesty first

No Hamilton office. No pretending.

Plenty of businesses list an address in a city they don't work from. We think that's a bad way to start a working relationship.

Google's Business Profile guidelines ban fake addresses. If a marketing company won't follow that rule for its own listing, what will it do with yours?

So we hold ourselves to the same rule we'd hold you to. We're in Etobicoke, and we say so.

Here's the part that matters for you. Your rankings don't depend on where our desk is. They depend on where your customers search from. That's why we check your rankings across a grid of points in your service area, from the lower city up to the Mountain, not from one spot on the map.

What that honesty looks like in your account

  • Your Business Profile shows your real address, or hides it if you're a service-area business that visits customers.
  • Your business name stays your business name. Not "Best Plumber Hamilton 24/7".
  • Rankings are checked where your customers are, whether that's Upper James or the east end of Stoney Creek.
  • Reports say what didn't work, too.

How we're different

Three things we do that most local SEO skips.

Every agency says it "does SEO". These three pillars are where our work actually looks different.

Keyword tracking that matches the map

We track the searches that lead to calls across your whole service area, using geo-grid rank tracking. One ranking from one spot tells you almost nothing. An HVAC company on Upper James can rank first on the Mountain and not show up at all in Dundas.

Defensive SEO

We protect the rankings you already have from spam links, deleted pages, broken redirects and careless edits. We once found a network of 60 spam domains pointing links at one small business site, and built a disavow file to deal with it.

AI implementation

Structured data, llms.txt and plain answers that ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews can quote. We even built a tool that shows the hidden searches ChatGPT runs when it answers a question, so we can see what it's looking for.

What we believe

A few things we won't budge on.

  • No ranking guarantees. Google itself says nobody can guarantee a #1 spot. Anyone who does is guessing, or selling something else.
  • Search Console over guesswork. Keyword tools estimate. Search Console measures. We've seen a tool put a site's traffic at about five times what Search Console showed.
  • One page, one job. "Drain cleaning Hamilton" and "water heater installation Ancaster" are different searches, so they get different pages.
  • Plain English. If we can't explain a fix in a sentence, we haven't understood it well enough yet.
  • No invented numbers. Every figure on this site comes from our own audits or a named source. We'll ask the same of your website.

What we usually find when we look

On September 20, 2026, we scored 35 live local-business websites on our audit rubric. The average was 34 out of 100.

The gaps were rarely exotic:

  • The wrong city or neighbourhood in page titles and headings
  • Template text left over from a different business
  • A robots.txt file telling Google to ignore the whole site
  • Pages deleted with no redirect, rankings and all

None of these needs a giant budget. They need someone to look.

Why Hamilton

Why we built a site just for Hamilton.

One city name covers a lot of very different maps.

Since amalgamation in 2001, "Hamilton" means the old city plus Stoney Creek, Ancaster, Dundas, Flamborough and Glanbrook. That's about 570,000 people (2021 census), spread from the lake to Binbrook.

Search results change across all of it. A customer on Concession Street and one in Waterdown can type the same words and see different businesses on the map.

That's exactly the problem our tracking is built for. It's also why a generic "Ontario SEO" plan rarely fits.

And Hamilton is close. It's near enough to know the difference between the lower city and the Mountain, and big enough that getting search right pays.
