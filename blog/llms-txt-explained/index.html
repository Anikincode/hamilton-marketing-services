What is llms.txt, and does your business need one?

It's a small text file that some people call essential and others call pointless. The truth sits in the middle, and it's a lot less dramatic than either side.

Kirill Anikin · October 30, 2026 · 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • llms.txt is a plain-text file at the root of a website. It summarizes the business and points AI tools to the most useful pages.
  • It's a community proposal, not an official standard. Google's guidance says you don't need any special file to appear in its AI features, and no big AI company has published a promise to use it.
  • We add one to every site we build anyway, because it's cheap, harmless, and forces a clear summary of the business.
  • It doesn't replace robots.txt, structured data or good service pages.

Someone told you your website needs an llms.txt file. Someone else told you it's a fad.

They're both a little bit right. Here's what llms.txt is, what it can and can't do, and how to decide whether it's worth ten minutes of your time. (It usually is, for reasons that have less to do with AI than you'd think.)

What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a text file that sits at the root of your website, the same place robots.txt lives. "LLM" stands for large language model, the technology behind tools like ChatGPT and Gemini.

The file is a short, structured summary of your site, written in Markdown, a simple text format. It usually has your business name, a sentence or two on what you do, and a list of your most important pages with a one-line description of each.

The idea is simple. A website is built for people: menus, images, pop-ups, scripts. An AI tool has to dig through all of that to find the facts. llms.txt hands it the facts on one page.

The format was put forward as a proposal, and you can read it at llmstxt.org.

An analogy that helps

Picture a renovation company's showroom on Wilson Street in Ancaster.

  • robots.txt is the sign on the staff door: who's allowed into which rooms.
  • Your sitemap is the floor plan: every room in the building.
  • llms.txt is the one-page brochure at the front desk: who we are, what we do, and where to look first.

A visitor can find their way around without the brochure. It just makes the first minute easier.

What goes in an llms.txt file for a local business?

Keep it short and factual. Here's what we include, with an example for an imaginary HVAC company in Hamilton:

SectionWhat to putExample
TitleYour exact business name, as it appears everywhere elseThe business name, spelled the same as on your Google Business Profile
SummaryOne or two sentences: what you do, for whom, whereFurnace, heat pump and AC installation and repair for homes in Hamilton, Stoney Creek, Ancaster and Dundas
ContactPhone, email, hoursThe same phone and hours shown on your site and listings
ServicesA link to each service page, with one line eachFurnace repair: diagnosis and repair of gas furnaces, all major brands
AreasA link to each area page, if you have themStoney Creek: service across the east end, including Winona and Fruitland
Key infoPricing, warranty, FAQ and about pagesLinks only, with a short description of each

This site publishes one too. It's generated automatically from the same page titles and descriptions you see in search results, so it can't drift out of step with the pages it describes.

Does llms.txt actually do anything?

Here's the honest answer: nobody outside the AI companies can say for sure.

Google's guidance on its AI features says there are no extra requirements to appear in AI Overviews, beyond normal SEO best practice. No special file is needed. And none of the big AI companies has published a commitment to read llms.txt when its assistant answers a question.

So if anyone tells you llms.txt will get you into ChatGPT, be sceptical. Nothing public supports that.

So why do we add one anyway?

  • It costs almost nothing. On the sites we build, it's part of the build, not an extra project.
  • It forces clarity. Writing a two-sentence summary of your business is harder than it sounds. That same summary belongs on your homepage, your Business Profile and your directory listings.
  • It does no harm. It doesn't block anything, change anything or slow anything down.
  • It's ready if things change. If AI tools start relying on it, you're already there.

What llms.txt is not

It's not robots.txt. llms.txt doesn't allow or block anything. If you want to control which AI crawlers can visit your site, that's done in robots.txt, and mistakes there can hide your whole site. Our article on robots.txt mistakes covers what to watch for.

It's not structured data. Schema markup sits inside each page and describes that page in a standard vocabulary. llms.txt is a separate summary of the whole site. They work well together. See schema markup for AI search.

It's not a substitute for content. If your llms.txt links to a "Services" page that's one paragraph long, the AI still finds one paragraph. The file points the way. The pages have to be worth visiting.

How to add one

  1. Write the summary first. Business name, what you do, where you work. Plain sentences, no slogans.
  2. List your key pages. Every service page, every area page, plus about, contact and pricing. Give each a one-line description.
  3. Save it as a plain text file named llms.txt.
  4. Upload it to the root of your domain, so it opens at yourdomain.ca/llms.txt. On WordPress or a static site, that's a simple upload. On some hosted website builders, putting a file at the root isn't straightforward, so ask whoever manages your site.
  5. Update it when things change. New service, new area, new phone number: update the file the same day.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Keyword stuffing. "Best plumber Hamilton cheap plumber Hamilton emergency plumber Hamilton" helps no one, human or machine.
  • Copying another site's file. We've found template text from other businesses, including a different city and a fake phone number, left on live websites. Don't repeat that mistake in a file nobody looks at.
  • Listing services you don't sell. We've audited a Business Profile with one category and a list of stock services the business didn't offer. The same mistake in llms.txt hands AI tools the wrong menu.
  • Claims you can't back up. Unsupported numbers on a homepage are already a risk. In a file written for machines to quote, they're worse.
  • Letting it go stale. An out-of-date llms.txt is worse than none at all.

FAQ

Is llms.txt required?

No. It's a proposal, not a requirement, and Google says you don't need special files to appear in its AI features. We add it because it's cheap, harmless and makes you write a clear summary of your business.

Will an llms.txt file get my business into ChatGPT?

Not on its own. ChatGPT names businesses it finds through web searches and can read clearly. Clear service pages, consistent details and reviews matter far more. llms.txt is a tidy extra on top.

Does llms.txt stop AI companies using my content?

No. It doesn't block anything. Controlling AI crawlers is done in robots.txt, and it's worth getting advice before you block anything, because the wrong rule can hide you from search too.

How often should I update llms.txt?

Whenever your services, areas, phone number or hours change. If it's generated from your pages automatically, as it is on this site, it updates whenever they do.
