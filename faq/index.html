Process

How the work happens

What happens in the free audit?

We review your website, your Google Business Profile and where you rank today. Then we send the three fixes that matter most, in plain English. What you do with them is up to you.

What do you do first once we start working together?

We start with your Business Profile categories, because your primary category decides which searches you can show up for. Then we crawl your site, group keywords by intent and plan one page per service. The full order is on our methodology page.

What do you need from me?

The things only you know: what you sell, where you work, which jobs you want more of and which you'd rather skip. Real photos of your work help. So does asking happy customers for reviews.

Will you need access to my Google accounts and website?

Usually, yes: Search Console, your Business Profile and your website editor. Add us as a user or manager, not as the owner. Whoever you hire, those accounts should stay in your name.

Can I do some of the work myself?

Yes. Plenty of owners handle their own review requests and photos. The free audit is written so you can act on it without us, or hand it to your web person.

Why do some companies charge so little for SEO?

Usually because something is missing. The common gaps: rankings checked from one spot only, template pages copied from another business, no redirects when pages change, and reports built on estimates instead of Search Console.

Is ad spend included in a Google Ads management fee?

No. Ad spend is what Google charges for clicks. The management fee is what you pay someone to run the account. A good quote shows both numbers separately.

Results

Timelines, rankings and proof

How long does SEO take to work?

Quick fixes, like a wrong category or a broken title, can show up within weeks. New pages and new rankings usually take a few months. Anyone promising page one in 30 days is selling something else.

Can you guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?

No. Google itself says nobody can guarantee a #1 ranking. What we can promise is clear work, done in the right order, and honest reporting on what it's doing for your calls.

How will I know SEO is working?

Watch calls, form leads and booked jobs first. Then rankings across your service area and the clicks Search Console reports. If rankings rise but calls don't, something on the page needs fixing, and we'll tell you.

Why do my customers see different results from me?

Because the map pack changes with the searcher's location. Someone searching "furnace repair" on the Mountain can see different businesses from someone in Dundas. That's why we track rankings on a grid across your service area, not from one spot.

A keyword tool says I get lots of traffic. Why does Search Console show less?

Keyword tools model traffic. Search Console measures it. We've seen a tool estimate a site's traffic at about five times what Search Console showed. Trust the measurement.

Local SEO

Google Maps and your Business Profile

What is the map pack?

It's the short list of businesses with a map that appears for searches like "emergency plumber Hamilton" or "plumber near me". It comes from Google Business Profiles, not websites, and it often sits above the regular results. Our local SEO work is built around it.

Can I show up on Google Maps without a storefront?

Yes. Service-area businesses, like plumbers or cleaners who go to the customer, can hide their address on their Business Profile and list the areas they serve instead. What you can't do is use a fake address.

Should I add "Hamilton" or keywords to my business name?

Not unless it's genuinely your business's real-world name. Google's guidelines ban keyword-stuffed business names, and listings that break the rules can be suspended.

How do I choose the right primary category?

Choose the category that describes the service you most want calls for, not the broadest one available. Your primary category decides which searches you can show up for, so it's the first thing we check.

How do I get more Google reviews?

Ask every happy customer, right after the job, with a direct link to your review form. Reply to every review, good or bad. Don't buy reviews or offer anything in return. That breaks Google's rules.

Do Business Profile posts help?

They keep your profile active and give searchers a reason to call: a seasonal service, a recent job, something they didn't know you offered. We write and schedule daily posts for clients who want them.

Technical

Websites, speed and indexing

Why isn't my website showing up on Google at all?

Check the basics first. We've seen a live site whose robots.txt file told Google to ignore every page. A stray "noindex" tag does the same thing. Our technical SEO work starts with exactly these checks.

Does page speed really matter?

It matters to the person holding the phone first. We've measured a site whose main content took 27.7 seconds to appear on mobile. Someone with a leaking water heater isn't going to wait that long.

Will a website redesign hurt my rankings?

It can, if old pages disappear without redirects. We've seen pages deleted with no redirect, including one that ranked for a search with real monthly volume. Map every old address to a new one before launch.

What does "Discovered – currently not indexed" mean?

Google knows the page exists but hasn't added it to its index. We've seen a site where Google indexed about 5 of its 48 pages. Making each page clearly different, and linking to it from your important pages, usually helps more than resubmitting.

Which website platform is best for SEO?

The platform matters less than what's on the pages. We build on Wix and as fast static sites, and we work on WordPress. Each has its own traps, and each can rank when it's set up properly.

AI search

ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews

What is AI search optimization?

Making your business easy for ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews to find, trust and recommend. That means clear answers, structured data and the same business details everywhere. More on our AI search optimization page.

How can I tell if ChatGPT recommends my business?

Ask it the way a customer would: "who's a good emergency plumber on Hamilton Mountain?" We also built a tool that shows the hidden searches ChatGPT runs to answer questions like that, so we can see where it looks.

What is llms.txt, and do I need one?

It's a plain text file that summarizes your site for AI tools. It's a newer, proposed standard, so treat it as a cheap extra, not a replacement for clear pages.

Is AI search a separate service from SEO?

For most local businesses, no. The work overlaps heavily: good pages, consistent business details and reviews help in both. Be wary of anyone selling it as a bolt-on with big promises.

Do Local Services Ads make sense for my business?

Only if you're in the trades. In Canada, Local Services Ads cover the trades only. If you're a plumber or HVAC company, they're worth a look alongside regular Google Ads. A restaurant or accountant can't use them.

Do you work with restaurants?

Yes. Restaurant search works differently: hours, menus, photos and reviews matter more than service pages. Someone searching "brunch Locke Street" wants to see the food and know you're open.

My trade isn't on your list. Can you still help?

Probably, if your customers are local and search before they call. The free audit will show us both whether we're a good fit.
