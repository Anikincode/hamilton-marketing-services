Why we set up conversion tracking before we spend a cent.

Running Google Ads without conversion tracking is like paying for a billboard and never asking callers where they heard about you.

We've audited Google Ads accounts running with no conversion tracking at all. Money went out every day. Nobody could say which clicks turned into calls.

It's an expensive mistake, and an easy one to avoid. So before we touch bids or budgets, we make sure the account can see what a lead looks like.

Then every report answers one plain question: what did a call from "furnace repair Hamilton" actually cost you?

What we track for a local business

  • Calls made straight from the ad
  • Calls from your website after someone clicked an ad
  • Quote and contact form submissions
  • Online bookings, if you take them

Campaign setup

How we build a Google Ads campaign for a Hamilton business.

Local search ads work when each ad matches one service, one area and one landing page. Here's what that looks like.

Keywords by service, not by trade

"Water heater replacement Hamilton" and "drain cleaning Stoney Creek" get their own ad groups, so the ad someone sees matches what they typed.

Targeting where you actually work

If you serve the Mountain and Ancaster but not Grimsby, your ads shouldn't show in Grimsby. Locations are set to your real service area.

Negative keywords

We block searches that waste money: jobs, courses, DIY and "free". Someone searching "plumber jobs Hamilton" wants a job, not a plumber.

Ads that run when you answer

If nobody picks up after hours, a call ad shown late at night is money spent on voicemail. Ad schedules follow your real hours.

The right landing page

An ad for furnace repair should land on your furnace repair page, not your homepage. Google also rates how relevant your landing page is to the ad.

Search terms, checked and pruned

We read the search terms report, the list of real searches that triggered your ads, and keep cutting the ones that don't fit.

An ad can only bring someone to the page. If your service pages are thin or slow, a website built around your services makes every click work harder.

Local Services Ads

Local Services Ads: an option for the trades.

Local Services Ads are the listings that can appear at the top of some local searches, showing a business name, rating and a call button.

In Canada they cover the trades only. A plumber or an HVAC company in Hamilton should look at them. A restaurant or an accountant can't use them, so regular search ads are the route there.

Two things make them different. You pay per lead rather than per click, and Google verifies the business before the ads run.

Reviews matter here too, because your rating shows right on the ad. If you're a trade, our pages on SEO for plumbers in Hamilton and HVAC SEO in Hamilton cover how ads and rankings fit together for your trade.

Search ads or Local Services Ads?

  • Search ads: any business, any service. Full control over keywords, ads and landing pages.
  • Local Services Ads: trades only in Canada. Pay per lead, with less control over which searches trigger them.
  • Both: a trade can run the two side by side, with tracking on each so you can compare what a lead costs.

Ads + SEO

Google Ads and SEO work better together.

Ads are fast. SEO is slow but keeps paying. You don't have to pick one forever.

  1. Ads first, for speed

    A new page for "basement waterproofing Hamilton" can take months to rank. An ad can show as soon as it's approved, so the phone rings while SEO builds.

  2. Let ad data choose your SEO targets

    Ads show which searches turn into calls and which just burn clicks. Those proven searches are the pages worth building and ranking.

  3. Pull back where organic takes over

    Once you rank well for a search, you can test lowering ad spend on it and watch whether total calls hold. If they do, that budget moves somewhere it's needed.

Think of ads as renting a billboard and SEO as owning the building. Most local businesses are better off with both, with the mix shifting as local SEO starts bringing in calls on its own. For the longer comparison, read SEO vs Google Ads: which should you pay for first?

Paid to Google for clicks, or for leads with Local Services Ads. You set the budget and can change it. A bigger budget buys more clicks, not better ones.

FAQ

Google Ads questions from Hamilton businesses.

How much should I spend on Google Ads in Hamilton?

Enough to get a steady flow of clicks on your most important services, and no more until tracking proves it's working. The right figure depends on your trade and your service area. We suggest a starting budget after looking at your keywords.

Do Google Ads work for small local businesses?

They can, when each ad is tied to a specific service and every call is tracked. They waste money when one ad points every search at the homepage and nobody measures the result.

Can I use Local Services Ads for my business?

In Canada, Local Services Ads cover the trades only. If you're a plumber, HVAC company or another trade, ask us to check. Other businesses use regular search ads.

Can you take over my existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We start by checking the conversion tracking, then the search terms you've been paying for. That's where leaks tend to show up first.

Why are my ads getting clicks but no calls?

Usually one of three things: the ads show for the wrong searches, they send people to a page that doesn't match, or the phone number is hard to tap on mobile. Tracking tells you which.

Should I stop SEO if my ads are working?

No. Ads stop the day the budget does. SEO builds something you keep. Run both, and shift the mix toward organic as your rankings grow.
