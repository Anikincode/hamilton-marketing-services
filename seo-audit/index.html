SEO audit in Hamilton: what's broken, and what to fix first

Why audit

An audit should end in a to-do list.

Most audit reports are a long list of red warnings, with no answer to the one question you care about: why isn't the phone ringing?

Automated tools flag everything. A missing image description on your logo can sit right next to a site Google can't index, looking just as urgent.

Our audit works the other way round. A person looks at your site the way Google and your customers do, then ranks the problems by what they're costing you.

When we scored 35 live local-business websites on Sep 20, 2026, the average was 34 out of 100. Most local sites have gaps. The useful question is which ones to fix first.

What we check

What our SEO audit looks at.

Every audit uses the same rubric, so the next audit shows exactly what changed.

Google Business Profile

Primary and secondary categories, services, hours, photos and reviews. The primary category alone decides which searches you can show for. More on Business Profile optimization.

Rankings, from your area

Where you show on the map and in the results, checked from points across your service area with BrightLocal, not from one spot downtown.

Indexing

Whether Google can find and keep your pages: robots.txt, noindex tags and the Search Console Pages report.

Speed on mobile

PageSpeed Insights on your key pages, with a plain explanation of what's slow and why.

Titles, headings and pages

One H1 per page, the right city in each title, one page per service, and no leftover template text from another business.

Risks

Spam links pointing at your site, deleted pages with no redirect, and two pages competing for the same search.

How we measure

Tools estimate. Search Console measures.

An audit is only as good as the data under it. We check our findings against Google's own numbers.

Keyword tools are good for finding searches and comparing difficulty. We use Ubersuggest for that. But their traffic figures are models, not measurements.

On one site we audited, a keyword tool estimated traffic about five times higher than Search Console showed. Search Console is Google's own record of your clicks and impressions, so that's the number we trust.

If you've never opened it, our guide to Google Search Console for business owners walks you through the reports that matter.

An audit nobody acts on is just paperwork

One business we audited had 9 of 10 service pages missing an H1 heading. It was still unfixed after four audits.

Finding problems is the easy half. That's why our audits rank every fix by what it costs you, so the first one is obvious.

What happens next

What happens after the audit.

The audit is a map, not the trip. Here's how the work moves from findings to fixes.

  1. You get the findings

    Plain English, ranked by what each problem costs you. Each one says what's wrong, where it is and what fixing it involves.

  2. You choose who fixes it

    Do it yourself, hand it to your web person, or have us do it. Our technical SEO and SEO content writing services pick up where the audit stops.

  3. Every audit is dated

    Each audit is saved with its date, so the next one shows what changed. Overwriting old reports would erase the proof of progress.

  4. We re-audit after fixes ship

    Auditing a site that hasn't changed only produces the same report twice. We'd rather spend that time on the fixes.

FAQ

SEO audit questions.

Is the free SEO audit really free?

Yes. You get the three fixes that matter most whether or not you hire us. We'd rather you fix the right thing yourself than pay anyone to fix the wrong one.

What's the difference between the free and the full audit?

The free audit finds the biggest problems. The full audit plans the work: categories, content gaps, keyword groups, the questions customers ask and a page-by-page plan. The full audit costs $1,000.

What do you need from me?

For the free audit, your website and business name, plus the services and areas you care about most. For a full audit, access to Search Console and your Business Profile helps a lot, because it gives us real data instead of estimates.

Can't I just use a free audit tool?

You can, and they're useful for spotting technical issues. What they can't do is tell you which problems are costing you calls in Hamilton, or check your Business Profile and map rankings across your area. That takes a person.

Will an audit improve my rankings?

Not on its own. Rankings move when the fixes go live. The audit makes sure the first fixes are the ones that count.
