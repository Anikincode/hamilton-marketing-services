Squarespace SEO

Squarespace SEO in Hamilton, honestly explained

Squarespace sites look great and are easy to run. Getting them found takes a few deliberate choices. Here's what matters, and where we can help.

Straight talk

Can a Squarespace site rank on Google?

Yes. The platform covers the basics. What holds most Squarespace sites back is the content and the setup, not the software.

A quick word about us first. We build websites on Wix and as static sites, and we work on WordPress. We don't build Squarespace sites, and we're not going to claim years of Squarespace projects.

What we bring is the SEO groundwork that applies on every platform: one page per service, titles written for real searches, consistent business details and fast pages. Squarespace gives you the settings to do all of it.

Hosting and SSL

Included and maintained for you. No plugins, no server updates.

An automatic sitemap

Squarespace builds your sitemap and keeps it current as you add pages.

Page SEO fields

Every page has its own SEO title, description and URL slug in its settings.

Mobile-ready templates

Templates adapt to phones out of the box, which matters because Google looks at the mobile version first.

Common gaps

Where Squarespace sites fall short in search.

Squarespace templates are built to look beautiful. That's the selling point. It's also the trap.

Pictures instead of words

A full-screen photo and one line of text looks lovely. But Google can't rank a photo for "interior designer Ancaster". Pages need words that say what you do and where.

One "Services" page for everything

Kitchen design, home staging and colour consultations on one page end up competing with each other. Give each service its own page.

Blank SEO titles

Leave the SEO title empty and Squarespace falls back on the page title, which is often just "Services" or "About".

Heavy banners and video

Big hero images and video backgrounds look great on a laptop and drag on a phone. Check PageSpeed Insights on mobile.

Hidden or moved pages

A page set to hide from search engines by accident, or a URL changed with no redirect, quietly drops out of Google.

No local details

No city in the titles, no service area on the page, and a phone number that doesn't match the Business Profile.

The fix for most of these is better pages. Here's how to write service pages that rank.

Page setup

Setting up a Squarespace page for search.

Every Squarespace page has an SEO tab in its settings. Fill it in on every page that matters, not just the homepage.

On the page itself, use one Heading 1, set from the text block's format menu, and name the service and area in it. Then add the words that answer what a buyer wants to know. A gallery is fine. Just put text around it.

On plans that include code injection, you can add structured data such as LocalBusiness or FAQ markup. Our schema starter pack has examples you can adapt. Check the result with Google's Rich Results Test.

Example: a brunch page for a James Street North restaurant

  • URL slug: /brunch
  • SEO title: Brunch on James Street North, Hamilton | Your Restaurant
  • SEO description: what you serve, when, and whether to book
  • Heading 1: Brunch on James Street North
  • On the page: the menu as real text, hours, and how to reserve

Redirects

Changing a page? Set up URL mappings first.

A redirect is a forwarding address for Google and for anyone with an old bookmark.

Squarespace handles redirects with a feature called URL mappings, found in your site settings. You enter the old path, the new path, and mark it 301, which means "moved for good".

You need one when you rename a page, merge two pages, drop a service, or move to Squarespace from another platform.

Skip it and the old page simply vanishes. We've audited sites that deleted pages with no redirect, including one that ranked for a search with real monthly volume. That traffic didn't move to the new page. It was just gone.

FAQ

Squarespace SEO questions.

Is Squarespace bad for SEO?

No. It covers the basics: a sitemap, SSL, mobile templates and per-page SEO settings. Sites that don't rank usually have too little text, one page for every service, or no local details.

How do I redirect an old URL in Squarespace?

Use URL mappings, found in your site settings. Add the old path and the new path, and mark it 301 for a permanent move.

Can I add schema to a Squarespace site?

On plans that include code injection, yes. You can add LocalBusiness or FAQ schema as JSON-LD, then check it with Google's Rich Results Test.

Should I switch from Squarespace to another platform for better SEO?

Not for SEO alone. Better pages and settings come first. If you move for other reasons, redirect every old URL so you keep what you've earned.

Do you build Squarespace websites?

No. We build on Wix and as fast static sites, and work on WordPress. On Squarespace, we audit and fix the SEO of the site you already have.
