Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile optimization for Hamilton businesses

Your Business Profile is often the first thing a Hamilton customer sees, before your website and sometimes instead of it. We set it up to show for the jobs you want, without breaking Google's rules.

Why it matters

Your profile is your shopfront on Google Maps.

Search "furnace repair Stoney Creek" on a phone. Before any website, you'll see a map, three businesses, their stars, their hours and a call button.

That's the Google Business Profile. A customer can call you straight from it without ever opening your website.

Think of it as your sign, your business card and your front window in one place that Google controls. You fill it in. Google decides who sees it.

Optimizing it isn't about tricks. It's about telling Google the truth about your business, completely and consistently.

What a strong profile has

  • The right primary category
  • Every real service listed, and nothing you don't sell
  • Accurate hours, phone number and service area
  • Real photos of real work
  • Recent posts
  • A steady flow of reviews, with replies

Start here

Your primary category decides which searches you can show for.

It's the first step of our method, and the setting we most often find wrong.

Google uses your primary category as its main clue to what you do. Choose "HVAC contractor" and you're in the running for heating and cooling searches. Choose something vaguer and you may never show for "AC repair Waterdown" at all.

Secondary categories cover the rest of your work. Then the services list fills in the detail: furnace repair, furnace installation, air conditioner service, and so on.

Each category and each service is a small signal. Get them right and they all point Google the same way.

What we found: a Business Profile with one category and a list of stock services the business doesn't sell. Every one of those services was a signal pointing Google the wrong way. Read how to choose your Google Business Profile categories.

Google's rules

What Google bans, and why shortcuts backfire.

A suspended profile disappears from Maps. These are the shortcuts that put a listing at risk.

  • Keyword-stuffed names. Your profile name should be your real business name, the one on your sign and invoices. "Your Name Plumbing" is fine. "Your Name Plumbing, Best Emergency Plumber Hamilton 24/7" breaks the rules.
  • Fake addresses. Virtual offices and mailboxes don't count. If you don't really operate from a location, it can't be your address.
  • Bought or filtered reviews. Paying for reviews, trading discounts for them, or only asking the customers you know are happy all go against Google's review policies.
  • Extra listings. One business gets one profile per real location. A profile for each town you drive to is not allowed.

Service-area businesses can hide their address

If you go to your customers, like most plumbers, cleaners and movers, you can hide your address and list the areas you serve instead. That's allowed, and it's often the right setup. More on service-area businesses on Google Maps.

FAQ

Google Business Profile questions.

Can I put "Hamilton" in my business name on Google?

Only if it's part of your real business name, the one on your signage, invoices and registration. Adding a city or a service to your name just for Google breaks the guidelines, and anyone can suggest an edit to remove it.

Should a service-area business hide its address?

If you serve customers at their homes and don't welcome them at your location, yes. Hide the address and list your service areas. If customers come to you, like a restaurant or an auto repair shop, show it.

How often should I post on my Business Profile?

Often enough that the profile looks alive. We offer daily posts, scheduled ahead through Local Viking. But posts sit on top of the basics. They won't fix a wrong category.

Can you remove a bad review?

Only Google can, and only when a review breaks its policies, such as spam or a review that isn't based on a real experience. You can report those. For honest bad reviews, a calm, helpful reply is the best answer, because future customers read it too.

Do you need my Google password?

No. You add us as a manager on the profile and you stay the owner. You can remove a manager at any time.

Is optimizing my profile the same as local SEO?

It's the biggest part, but not all of it. Local SEO also covers your website's local pages, your listings on other sites and tracking across your area. See local SEO in Hamilton for the full picture.
