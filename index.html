The short version

3 moves that win local search in Hamilton.

If you only did three things this quarter, these are the three.

  1. Get your Google Business Profile right

    The right primary category, every real service listed, and steady reviews. This one profile decides whether you show on Maps at all.

  2. Give every service its own page

    "Furnace repair Stoney Creek" and "AC installation Hamilton" are different searches. One page trying to rank for both usually ranks for neither.

  3. Make your expertise easy to quote

    Clear answers, real prices and plain facts about your business. That's what AI assistants pick up when someone asks who to call.

SEO and ads

Why SEO keeps paying when the ads stop.

Google Ads are like renting a billboard. The calls stop the day you stop paying.

SEO is more like owning the building. It takes longer to set up, but a page that ranks keeps bringing in calls month after month. For most local businesses the best answer is both: ads for speed while SEO builds, then less ad spend as organic calls grow.

FAQ

Common questions.

How much does SEO cost in Hamilton?

It depends on how much needs fixing and how competitive your trade is. We quote after the free audit, which tells you what you actually need before you spend anything. See our pricing and the full cost guide.

How long does SEO take to work?

Quick fixes, like correcting your Google Business Profile or a broken title, can show up within weeks. New pages and new rankings usually take a few months. Anyone who promises page one in 30 days is selling something else.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Google itself warns that nobody can guarantee a ranking. What we can promise is clear work, done properly, and honest reporting on what it's doing for your calls and leads.

Do you have an office in Hamilton?

No, and we won't pretend to. We're based in Etobicoke, Toronto. We work with Hamilton businesses by phone, email and video call, and we track your rankings from inside your service area, not from Toronto.

Do I need a Google Business Profile if I already have a website?

Yes. Your website helps you rank in the regular results, but the map pack comes from your Google Business Profile. For most local searches, the map shows up first.

What is AI search, and do I need to worry about it?

More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews who to call. Those tools pull from websites, reviews and business listings. If your information is clear and consistent everywhere, you're far more likely to be named. Read more on AI search optimization.

Do you work with businesses outside Hamilton?

Yes. We work across Hamilton, Stoney Creek, Ancaster, Dundas, Waterdown and Binbrook, plus Burlington and Grimsby. We also work with businesses across the GTA.

What happens in the free audit?

We look at your website, your Google Business Profile and where you rank today, then send you the three fixes that will make the biggest difference. You can do them yourself, have your web person do them, or hire us. No pressure either way.
