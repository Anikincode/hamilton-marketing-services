Privacy policy

What we collect when you contact us, what we do with it, and how to get it changed or deleted. In plain English.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This page explains how Hamilton Marketing Services handles personal information. The short version: we collect what you choose to send us, we use it to reply to you, and we don't sell it or track you around the web.

Who we are

Hamilton Marketing Services is based in Etobicoke, Toronto, Ontario. When this policy says "we" or "us", it means Hamilton Marketing Services. Kirill Anikin is responsible for how we handle personal information.

Questions about this policy go to kirill.s.anikin@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you give us. That happens in three ways:

  • Our forms. When you request an audit or contact us, the form asks for things like your name, email, phone number, business name, website and a message. Only some fields are required.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.
  • Phone calls. If you call (647) 982-2545, we see your phone number and anything you tell us.

We don't ask for payment details, passwords or sensitive personal information through this website. Please don't send them.

How the contact form works

Our forms are processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form service. When you press send, FormSubmit receives what you typed and forwards it to our email inbox.

That means your form details pass through FormSubmit and our email provider. These services may store or process data outside Canada, and they handle it under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use advertising trackers, tracking pixels or analytics cookies. We don't follow you around the web, and we don't build advertising profiles.

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser types, to keep the site secure and running. Our fonts load from our own site, not from a third party.

If we ever add analytics, we'll update this page first.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your message or call.
  • To prepare the free audit or quote you asked for.
  • If you become a client, to do the work we agreed and to keep in touch about it.

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. We won't add you to a mailing list unless you ask to be added.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and client records only as long as we need them to reply to you, do the work we agreed and meet our tax and legal obligations. Then we delete them.

You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time (see below).

Your rights under PIPEDA

Canada's federal privacy law, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), gives you rights over your personal information. You can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that's wrong.
  • Withdraw your consent, and ask us to delete your information where we aren't required to keep it.
  • Complain to us, and if you're not satisfied, to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

To make any of these requests, email kirill.s.anikin@gmail.com. We may need to confirm who you are before we act on a request.

How we protect it

We keep your information in password-protected accounts, and only the people who need it to reply to you or do your work can see it.

Children

This website is for business owners. It isn't aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other websites, such as Google's help pages. Their privacy policies apply once you leave our site.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Email kirill.s.anikin@gmail.com or call (647) 982-2545. Hamilton Marketing Services, Etobicoke, Ontario.
