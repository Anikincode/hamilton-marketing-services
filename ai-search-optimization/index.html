AI search

AI search optimization: get named when customers ask AI who to call

More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews who to call. We make your business easy for those tools to find, understand and recommend, on top of solid SEO.

What it is

What AI search optimization means for a local business.

Ask ChatGPT for a good furnace repair company in Stoney Creek and you'll often get a short list of names, each with a line about why.

If you're not on that list, that customer may never see you.

AI search optimization is the work that makes your business easy for these tools to find, understand and trust. You'll also hear it called GEO or AEO. Here's AEO vs GEO vs SEO in plain English.

The labels are new. The work underneath is mostly good SEO, done carefully.

Where customers ask

  • ChatGPT, which can search the web before it answers
  • Google's AI Overviews, the summary at the top of many Google results
  • Gemini, Google's AI assistant

How it works

Where AI assistants get their answers.

They don't pull a list of businesses out of thin air. They read the web: your website, your Business Profile, review sites, directories and anything else written about you.

When ChatGPT searches the web to answer a question, it runs its own searches behind the scenes. The person asking usually doesn't see them.

We built a tool that shows those hidden searches. So when someone asks for the best emergency plumber on Hamilton Mountain, we can see what ChatGPT actually looked up, and whether your pages turned up.

That tells us which pages you're missing and which facts need to be clearer. Instead of guessing what the assistant wants, we can see it.

What gives you a fair chance

  • It can find you. Your pages are indexed and not blocked.
  • It can understand you. Clear services, areas and prices on the page.
  • It can trust you. The same name, phone and details everywhere, plus real reviews.
  • It can quote you. Short, direct answers it can lift into a reply.

What we do

What we work on.

AI implementation is one of our three pillars. In practice, it comes down to six things.

Structured data

Schema markup that states your business name, services, areas, hours and FAQs in a format machines read reliably. See schema markup for AI search.

llms.txt

A plain-text summary of your site for AI tools. It's a newer, proposed standard, and not every tool reads it yet. It's quick to add, so we add it, but we won't sell it as a magic switch.

Consistent details

One name, one phone number and the same details on your site, your profile and every listing. We've seen one website show three different review counts. When your site disagrees with itself, nothing reading it knows which to repeat.

Quotable answers

Short, direct answers to the questions customers ask, written so a sentence still makes sense when it's lifted out of the page. That's part of our SEO content writing.

Crawler access

Checking that robots.txt and site settings aren't blocking the crawlers you want. We once found a live site whose robots.txt blocked all indexing.

Facts you can back up

No made-up figures. We found one homepage full of unsupported, made-up-looking numbers. If an assistant repeats them, they're out there with your name on them.

Example

What a quotable answer looks like.

Here's the difference between copy an assistant can't use and copy it can.

Hard to quote

"We offer comprehensive heating and cooling solutions for all your residential and commercial needs across the region."

No service, no place, no fact. There's nothing to repeat.

Easy to quote

"We repair and replace gas furnaces in Hamilton, Stoney Creek and Ancaster. Repairs start at [your price]. Call [your number], [your hours]."

Service, places, price and contact in one short paragraph. The brackets are where your real details go.

It's the same writing that wins over a customer reading on a phone. Plain facts work for people and for machines.

How we measure it

How we check whether it's working.

AI answers aren't a ranking you can screenshot. Ask the same question twice and you can get a different list. So we measure carefully, and we tell you what we can and can't see.

  1. See what assistants say now

    We ask the questions your customers ask, like "who does furnace repair in Ancaster?", and record which businesses come up.

  2. See what they searched

    Our ChatGPT query tool shows the hidden searches behind each answer.

  3. Fix the facts

    Business Profile, listings, schema, llms.txt and your key pages, so your details match everywhere. Most of this overlaps with local SEO.

  4. Write the answers

    Clear pages and FAQs for the questions that bring in work.

  5. Recheck over time

    The same questions, asked again later, read alongside Search Console and your calls.

FAQ

AI search questions.

Is AI search replacing Google?

Nobody can say how fast things will shift. People still search Google and use Maps every day, and AI tools are growing alongside them. The good news is that the same work helps in both places.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No, and nobody honest can. Google says nobody can guarantee a #1 ranking, and AI answers are even less predictable. What we can do is make your business easy to find, understand and trust.

What is llms.txt, and do I need one?

It's a plain-text file that summarizes your site for AI tools. It's a proposed standard, so not every tool uses it yet. It's worth having because it's quick to add, but it won't fix weak pages. Read llms.txt explained.

Does schema markup help with AI search?

It helps machines read your details without guessing: your services, areas, hours and FAQs. It doesn't force any tool to mention you, but it removes a reason not to.

Is AI search optimization a separate service?

Usually it's part of our SEO work, not a separate product. Most of it, like your Business Profile, consistent details and clear service pages, is good local SEO anyway. Be wary of anyone selling it as a mysterious extra.
