SEO on Hamilton Mountain for businesses in the upper city

Hamilton Mountain has its own name, its own main roads and its own map-pack results. Here's how to win the upper city without giving up on the rest of Hamilton.

Get a free SEO audit(647) 982-2545

Where it is

The Mountain is the top half of Hamilton

The Niagara Escarpment cuts the city in two. Below it are downtown and the lower city. Above it is the upper city, and that's what locals mean by Hamilton Mountain.

Upper James Street and Upper Wentworth are the big roads. Concession Street and Limeridge Mall are places nearly everyone on the Mountain can point to.

The Linc carries traffic across the Mountain. When people need the lower city, the Jolley Cut and the Claremont Access are the usual ways down.

Much of the Mountain is residential. That makes it home-services ground: when a furnace quits, a drain backs up or a dishwasher floods the kitchen in a house up here, someone reaches for their phone.

Who's searching

What upper-city customers look for

The searches below are examples to show the pattern, not volume figures. We measure the real ones for your business before building anything.

Something broke, today

"Emergency plumber Hamilton Mountain." "Furnace repair near me." These people want a truck at the door, not a research project.

Planned projects

A basement renovation, new landscaping or an interior designer. Slower decisions with more reading: reviews, photos of past jobs and a website that explains the process.

Drivers on the main roads

An auto shop on Upper James gets found by people who are already driving Upper James. "Brakes near me" from a phone at a red light is that search.

How search behaves here

Same search, different results, a few streets apart

The map pack follows the person searching. Someone near Concession Street and someone by Limeridge Mall can type identical words and get a different top three.

So "where do I rank?" has no single answer. A plumber based off Upper James might be first for the homeowners around it and invisible to customers on the far side of the Mountain.

Then there's the escarpment. Customers in the lower city are a separate group of searchers, with their own nearby businesses. A Mountain company that wants downtown work has to earn it with reviews and strong pages; ticking a wider service area doesn't do it alone.

Searches come in three flavours here: with the area ("roofer Hamilton Mountain"), with the city ("roofer Hamilton") and with nothing at all, where the phone's location fills the gap. You want to be the answer to all three.

One ranking from one spot is a guess

Most ranking reports check from a single point. That tells you almost nothing about a business whose customers are spread across the upper city.

We use geo-grid tracking in BrightLocal: the same search checked from a grid of points across your area, including a few below the escarpment, so you can see exactly where your reach runs out.

The plan

First moves for a Mountain business

In this order, because each one makes the next one work better.

  1. Decide how you appear on the map

    Plenty of Mountain trades run from a home or a small shop and go to the customer. If nobody visits you, Google lets you hide the address and list the areas you serve. If customers do come in, show it.

  2. List every real service, and nothing else

    Your Business Profile services should match the jobs you want: drain cleaning, water heater installation, whatever pays. Leave off anything you don't actually do.

  3. Only build a Mountain page if you have something to say

    A "Hamilton Mountain" page that copies your homepage competes with it. We've seen a new page take over six searches from a homepage, and all six dropped. A Mountain page earns its place with jobs done up here, the roads you cover and the questions these customers ask. Why one page should own one search.

  4. Track the searches that bring calls

    Not hundreds of keywords. The handful, like "emergency plumber Hamilton Mountain", that turn into booked jobs, checked across the grid every month.

For the record: nobody from Hamilton Marketing Services works out of an office on the Mountain. The site is run from Etobicoke. We do the work remotely, and the grid is how we see your rankings from the upper city rather than from Toronto. FAQ

Hamilton Mountain SEO questions

Should my website say "Hamilton Mountain" or just "Hamilton"?

Both, each where it's true. Your main service pages should target Hamilton. Mention the Mountain where it describes where you're based or where you work, in the words customers use.

My shop is on the Mountain. Why don't I show up for people downtown?

Because the map pack favours businesses near each searcher, and the lower city has its own. Downtown is covered on our downtown Hamilton SEO page. Reaching it from up here takes more reviews, clearer pages and patience.

I work from home on the Mountain. Do I have to show my address?

No. Google lets service-area businesses hide the address and show the areas they cover instead. What you can't do is list a fake address or a mailbox to look closer to customers. That breaks the guidelines and can get a listing suspended.

How will I know where I rank across the Mountain?

You'll get a map. Each point shows where you placed for a search from that spot, so you can see the streets where you're in the top three and the ones where you drop off.
