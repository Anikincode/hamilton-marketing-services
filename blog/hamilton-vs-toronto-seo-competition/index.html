SEO competition in Hamilton vs Toronto: what the numbers show

We measured search demand and the strength of the sites ranking across Ontario's biggest markets. Of the cities we looked at, Hamilton came out as the easiest one worth competing in.

Kirill Anikin · October 30, 2026 · 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • Toronto has about five times Hamilton's demand for SEO services: 5,503 searches a month against 1,138.
  • But the field is far weaker in Hamilton. The top three results for "seo hamilton" had a domain authority of 16, 10 and 11. Toronto's results ranged from 19 to 86.
  • Every "SEO + city" search swings about tenfold between seasons, so compare 12-month averages, not one month.
  • The map pack follows real location. A Toronto business is unlikely to win Hamilton's map pack without a real Hamilton address, and the same goes the other way.

Toronto has about five times the searches. It also has a field that's far harder to break into.

In October 2026 we measured how often people search for SEO help in Ontario's biggest cities, and how strong the websites already ranking are. We did it to decide where competing makes sense. The answer was clear, and it wasn't Toronto.

Here are the numbers, what they mean, and what they can't tell you.

What we measured

We pulled search volumes from Ubersuggest at Canada level, using 12-month averages rather than the latest month. Ubersuggest has no Ontario-level setting, so measuring every city at Canada level keeps them comparable.

For each city we added three phrasings together: "seo services [city]", "seo [city]" and "seo company [city]". All three mean the same thing, "find me someone local to do my SEO", so they belong on one page and count as one market.

Then we looked at who's already ranking. For that we used domain authority (DA), a score SEO tools give a website based mostly on its links. Google doesn't use DA, but it's a fair rough gauge of how established the competition is. Higher means harder to beat.

On demand alone, Toronto wins easily. If search volume were the whole story, everyone would chase Toronto. It isn't the whole story.

Difficulty: who's already ranking

Demand tells you how big the prize is. The sites already ranking tell you how hard it is to take.

That's the gap. In Toronto, Brampton and Oakville, established agencies and multi-city templates fill the top. In Hamilton, the top three results for the most-searched phrasing are sites a well-built local page can realistically compete with.

Putting demand and difficulty together

Think of it like choosing where to open a shop. Toronto is a busy downtown street where every storefront is already taken by a big chain. Hamilton is a busy street with a few small shops and room to stand out.

  • Toronto: the biggest prize and the hardest field.
  • Mississauga: about 2.5 times Hamilton's demand, with a medium-difficulty field.
  • Hamilton: about a fifth of Toronto's demand, with the weakest field of the big markets we measured.
  • Burlington: smaller, and easy if the page leads with "SEO company" rather than "SEO services".

That's why, among the cities we measured, Hamilton was the easiest market worth competing in. The demand is real, and the bar is low enough to clear.

The seasonal swing

One more thing jumped out. Every "SEO + city" search swings about tenfold over the year: high from September to January, low from April to August.

"seo services toronto" ran from 6,600 searches in its busiest month down to 480 in its quietest. If you looked at one month, you could conclude almost anything. That's why everything above uses 12-month averages.

For a business, the lesson is timing. Pages need time to get indexed and rank, so build them before the busy season starts, not in the middle of it.

What this means if your business is in Hamilton

Our research measured searches for SEO services, our own industry. Your trade has its own market, and it may look different. But the method works for any business.

Measure your own market the same way

Take your main service, add the city, and check a few phrasings: "furnace repair Hamilton", "Hamilton furnace repair", "furnace repair near me". Use 12-month averages. Then search the main one and look at what's ranking: real local businesses, directories, or big national sites? Our guide to local keyword research walks through it.

Don't chase the map pack where you don't have a real location

The map pack depends heavily on where the searcher is and where the business is. A Hamilton business is unlikely to show in Toronto's map pack, and a Toronto business is unlikely to show in Hamilton's, without a real local address.

Google's Business Profile guidelines ban fake addresses, and breaking them can get a real listing suspended. If you want to reach a neighbouring city, an organic page with real substance is the honest route. That's how we approach Burlington, for example: a separate city next door, covered with a page of its own, like our Burlington SEO page. The difference between the two kinds of results is explained in map pack vs organic results.

Search the city, not just the neighbourhood

Within Hamilton, area names carry little search volume of their own in our industry. "SEO" searches with Stoney Creek, Ancaster or Grimsby come in at 0 to 10 a month. People search the city name. Area pages can still help in the map pack, but the city-wide page is where most of the demand sits.

What the numbers can't tell you

We'd rather show the limits than oversell the data.

  • The search results were captured at Canada level, not from inside each city. Real local results will show more local businesses. Read the DA figures as a comparison between cities, not as exact local rankings.
  • Some results were partly hidden. In several cities, many of the ranking domains couldn't be read, so those verdicts lean on page titles.
  • Volumes are modelled. Ubersuggest estimates them from Google Ads ranges. Compare cities with each other, and don't treat any number as a visitor forecast.
  • Difficulty scores are shaky at low volume. Below about a hundred searches a month, the tool's difficulty score means very little, so we judged difficulty from the actual top 10 instead.

Before you build a page off the back of research like this, do a quick check of the main search from inside the city itself. Then, once the page is live, Search Console shows what really happens.

Frequently asked questions

Is SEO easier in Hamilton than in Toronto?

For SEO services, yes, in our October 2026 research. Toronto has about five times the demand, but its top results range up to DA 86. Hamilton's top three for "seo hamilton" were DA 16, 10 and 11. Your own trade may differ, so measure it.

Should a Hamilton business also target Toronto searches?

Usually not at first. You won't appear in Toronto's map pack without a real location there, and Toronto's organic results are much harder to break into. Win Hamilton and the areas next to it first.

Can a Toronto business rank in Hamilton?

In the organic results, with a genuinely useful Hamilton page, it's possible. In the map pack, not without a real Hamilton location. Faking an address breaks Google's Business Profile guidelines.

How do I check how competitive my own trade is in Hamilton?

Look up your main service plus "Hamilton" in a keyword tool using 12-month averages, then search it and note what ranks. Many big national sites means harder. Small local sites and directories means more room for you.
