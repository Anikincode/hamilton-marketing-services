Wix SEO

Wix SEO in Hamilton, from people who build on Wix

Wix can rank. Most Wix sites just aren't set up to. We build on Wix and fix Wix sites, so we know where the traps are.

The honest answer

Is Wix good for SEO?

Short answer: yes, when it's set up properly.

Wix still has a reputation for bad SEO. But most of the problems we find on Wix sites aren't Wix's fault. They're settings nobody changed and shortcuts nobody cleaned up.

We build on Wix ourselves, and we've audited many local-business Wix sites. Here's what the platform gets right.

Page-level SEO settings

Every page has its own title, meta description and URL slug, built in. No plugin needed.

A sitemap done for you

Wix creates and updates your sitemap automatically, and can connect your site to Google Search Console.

Hosting and security handled

No plugins to update and no server to patch. That alone removes a whole class of problems other platforms have.

Owners can edit

You can change a price, add project photos or write a post yourself, without waiting on a developer.

Wix traps

The Wix SEO traps we find again and again.

None of these are hard to fix. They're just easy to miss when you're running a business.

Big text that isn't a heading

In Wix, a heading only counts as an H1 when its text is set to the Heading 1 tag. Making text big and bold tells Google nothing. We've audited a site with 9 of 10 service pages missing an H1.

Default page titles

Leave the settings alone and a page title is just the page name plus your site name. "Services | Your Business" tells Google nothing about "furnace repair Stoney Creek".

Duplicated pages carrying old text

Duplicating a page is the quick way to add a service. It's also how a paragraph about one city ends up on a page about another. We've found a fake phone number from a cloned template on 19 pages of a live site.

Deleted pages with no redirect

Delete a page and anyone who finds the old address, Google included, hits an error. We've seen a deleted page that ranked for a search with real monthly volume, gone with no redirect.

The blog feed

Wix's blog feed loads posts in a way some crawl tools don't follow, so an audit may flag your posts as "orphans" with no links pointing at them. Sometimes that's just the tool. Either way, link your best posts from the service pages they support.

Heavy pages on mobile

Animations, video backgrounds, big images and extra apps all add weight. A homeowner on Upper James with a broken furnace won't wait. Test pages in PageSpeed Insights on mobile, not just on your desktop.

Page settings

How to set up a Wix page so Google knows what it's for.

Each Wix page has SEO settings of its own. This is where most of the work happens, and it only takes a few minutes per page once you know what goes where.

The rule is simple: one page, one service, one area. Then make every setting on that page say the same thing.

Wix also lets you add your own structured data in a page's advanced SEO settings. That's where LocalBusiness or FAQ markup goes when the built-in markup isn't enough. Our schema starter pack has copy-ready examples.

Finally, keep your business name, phone and service area identical to your Google Business Profile. That consistency is what local SEO is built on. For what to write on the page itself, read how to write service pages that rank.

Example: a furnace repair page in Stoney Creek

  • URL slug: /furnace-repair-stoney-creek
  • Title tag: Furnace Repair Stoney Creek | Your Business
  • Meta description: what you fix, where, and why someone should call you
  • H1 on the page: Furnace repair in Stoney Creek, set to the Heading 1 tag
  • Indexing: the page is allowed to appear in search results

Redirects

Wix redirects: the step most redesigns skip.

Wix's URL Redirect Manager is simple to use. The problem is that people forget it exists.

A 301 redirect is a forwarding address. It tells Google a page has moved for good and where to find it. You need one whenever an address changes:

  • Moving to Wix from another platform, where every old address is different
  • Renaming a page, for example to add "Hamilton" to the slug
  • Dropping a service you no longer offer
  • Merging two pages that compete for the same search. We've seen a new page take over from a homepage on six searches, and all six dropped.

Before any of these, list the old URLs, decide where each one goes, and add the redirects. Then watch Search Console for errors afterwards. The full process is in our guide to redesigning a website without losing rankings.

Stay or move

Should you stay on Wix?

Most local service businesses can rank on Wix. We build on it ourselves, so we're not going to tell you to throw yours away.

A rebuild to "fix SEO" usually isn't needed. Fixing the settings costs less and keeps what you've already earned.

We can audit your Wix site and hand you a fix list, or make the fixes ourselves in your Wix editor. Pricing is quoted after the free audit.

If the site really is past saving, see how we approach web design in Hamilton.

When moving off Wix makes sense

  • The site is still slow on phones after the apps, videos and heavy images are cleaned up.
  • You need features Wix can't do cleanly.
  • You never edit the site yourself and want the fastest pages possible. That's when we'd suggest a static site.

FAQ

Wix SEO questions.

Is Wix bad for SEO?

No. Wix gives you page-level titles, descriptions, URL slugs, redirects and an automatic sitemap. Most Wix sites that don't rank have setup problems, not platform problems.

How do I add a 301 redirect in Wix?

Use the URL Redirect Manager in the SEO tools of your Wix dashboard. Enter the old address and choose the new page. Do it before you delete or rename a page, not after.

Why does an SEO tool say my Wix blog posts are orphans?

Some crawl tools don't follow the way the Wix blog feed loads posts, so they can't see the links. Check before you panic, and link important posts from your service pages anyway.

Can you work on my existing Wix site?

Yes. You can add us as a collaborator through Wix's roles and permissions, so you never have to share your password.

Do I need a paid Wix plan for SEO?

To use your own domain name, yes. A free Wix site lives on a Wix web address, which is a weak base for a local business that wants to be found.

Is the Wix SEO checklist enough?

It's a fine start for the basics. It won't tell you which searches deserve their own page, or catch a duplicated paragraph naming the wrong city.
