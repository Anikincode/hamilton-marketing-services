Two seasons, two searches

Your year has two peaks, and search follows them

An HVAC company is really two businesses sharing one phone line. In January it's furnaces. In July it's air conditioning. The searches change with the weather, and so should your SEO.

Heating season

Cold-weather searches are often emergencies. A family with no heat in Binbrook isn't comparing ten websites. They call whoever looks reliable on the map.

  • "furnace repair Stoney Creek"
  • "no heat Hamilton Mountain"
  • "furnace not turning on"
  • "new furnace cost Ancaster"

Cooling season

AC breakdowns in a heat wave are urgent too. But installations, heat pumps and ductless systems are researched for weeks, often with several quotes.

  • "AC repair Hamilton"
  • "central air installation Waterdown"
  • "heat pump installer Dundas"
  • "ductless mini split Hamilton"

Timing is the whole game. A furnace page published in December is late. Google needs time to find a new page and trust it, so seasonal pages should be live and indexed before the season starts, then refreshed every year rather than replaced.

Google Business Profile

Categories for a heating and cooling company

The primary category is the biggest single choice on your profile. It tells Google which map searches you belong in.

For a company doing both furnaces and air conditioning, "HVAC contractor" is the usual primary. Then add the secondaries that match work you really do:

  • Heating contractor and Air conditioning contractor. They cover the two halves of the year in plain words.
  • Furnace repair service and Air conditioning repair service. For the repair-heavy shop that lives on service calls.
  • Air duct cleaning service. Only if you own the equipment and sell it as a real service.
  • Gas installation service. Only if you run gas lines for ranges, fireplaces or barbecues.

Pick the nearest match Google shows you during setup, because its category names shift from time to time.

Use posts to change seasons on the profile

Your categories stay put all year. Your posts and photos shouldn't. Lead with furnace tune-ups and "no heat" service in the fall, then switch to AC checks and heat pump quotes in the spring.

Fill in the services list too, one entry per real job: furnace installation, humidifiers, thermostats, AC repair. Each one is another hint to Google about what you do. Our Business Profile optimization covers the full setup.

Service pages

Repair and installation are different searches

Someone typing "furnace repair Stoney Creek" wants a truck today. Someone typing "furnace installation Stoney Creek" wants options, prices and a quote. One page can't serve both well.

Furnace repair

Symptoms, what a service call covers, and how to reach you after hours if you really offer that.

Furnace installation

Sizing, efficiency, timelines and what happens to the old unit. This is where planned buyers compare.

AC repair

Not cooling, frozen lines, strange noises. Built to rank by the first hot week, not the third.

Heat pumps

A long-research purchase. Explain how a heat pump works in an Ontario winter and whether it replaces the furnace or works beside it.

Ductless mini splits

Popular for additions, basements and older homes without ducts. A separate search from central air.

Maintenance plans

Your off-season revenue. A clear page lets you sell tune-ups in spring and fall when the phones are quiet.

If you help customers with rebate paperwork, say so on the relevant page and date the details, since programs change. More on how we write these in service pages that rank.

Reviews and ads

Winning the cold snap

When the temperature drops, every HVAC company in Hamilton wants the same calls on the same night. Two things tip the balance.

Reviews that name the work

HVAC reviews often mention the technician by name, how fast someone came out, and whether the bill matched the quote. Those details are exactly what the next customer reads.

Have technicians ask at the end of every job, and send the review link by text the same day. Reply to every review, especially the unhappy ones, with facts and no arguing.

Local Services Ads and search ads

Heating and cooling is one of the Canadian Local Services Ads categories, so you can pay per lead and appear above the regular ads once Google verifies your business.

Regular search ads fill in the rest, especially for installation terms. The rule we follow: conversion tracking first, then spend. Read SEO vs Google Ads for how the two work together, or see our Google Ads service.

Common website problems

Where HVAC websites lose calls

Most HVAC sites we look at have decent services. The problems are in how the pages are labelled, built and kept up.

Titles nobody clicks

"Services | Company Name" tells a searcher nothing. Our own site once had a page at average position 3 to 5 in Search Console with zero clicks from 233 impressions. The ranking was fine. The title wasn't.

Brochure copy

Product descriptions pasted from the manufacturer appear on hundreds of other sites. Google has no reason to rank yours over theirs.

Last year's promotions

An expired offer or old rebate in the header tells visitors nobody is minding the site. Date your offers and remove them when they end.

Search Console shows which pages get seen but not clicked, for free. Our Search Console guide for business owners shows where to look.

FAQ

HVAC SEO questions

When should furnace and AC pages go live?

Well before the season. Aim to have furnace pages indexed by early fall and AC pages by spring. Then update the same pages each year instead of making new ones, so they keep the rankings they've earned.

Should repair and installation share one page?

No. "AC repair Hamilton" and "AC installation Hamilton" come from different people with different questions. Give each its own page and link them to each other.

Which primary category should an HVAC company use?

"HVAC contractor" fits most companies that do heating and cooling. A shop that does mostly furnaces might test "Heating contractor". Change one thing at a time and track the map results before and after.

Are Local Services Ads available for HVAC in Hamilton?

HVAC is among the trades Google offers them for in Canada. Check the current categories when you apply, since Google adjusts the list, and expect a verification step before ads run.

What do we do in the quiet months?

That's when the SEO work gets done. Spring and fall are the time to write new pages, fix the site and collect reviews, so you're ready when the phones get busy again.
